# QuestPlanner

## 1.1.0

- New **Upgrades** tab: quests whose rewards are gear upgrades for you, best first. Scored with Pawn when it's installed, otherwise by item level against what you wear. Look-ahead (default 3 levels above you), Available now filter, Rescan
- Rescans by itself after gear changes, level-ups and turn-ins
- Upgrade arrows on list rows, an Upgrade chip on quest pages, and the upgrade reward marked in the reward tiles
- `/qp upgrades`
- First CurseForge release

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Browse, Near Me and My Plan tabs with level, race/class, completed, gear and zone filters
- Quest pages on the quest-log parchment: level and status chips, quest giver, objectives and turn-in with coordinates and one-click waypoints
- Live objective progress bars for quests in your log
- Rewards with XP at your level (and the share of your level bar), money, reputation, and reward tiles with quality borders and Pawn upgrade arrows
- Quest chain timeline with clickable prerequisites and follow-ups
- Per-character plans with notes, sorting and reordering, backed up against lost SavedVariables
- Minimap button, addon compartment, `/qp` commands, Alt-click quest links
